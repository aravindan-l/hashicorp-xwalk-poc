Recorded Webinar

EMEA Partner Masterclass: HashiCorp & The Cloud Operating Model

The HashiCorp suite of tools seeks to provide solutions for each layer of the cloud to enable enterprises in their digital transformation and to make the shift to the cloud operating model. Along with our partners, we help the world's largest companies to deliver applications faster by helping transition from manual processes and ITIL practices to self-service automation and DevOps practices.

During this first of four masterclass sessions exclusively for our partners in EMEA, we will explore the overall HashiCorp vision, the journey from open source to enterprise, and how to better position HashiCorp to your customers in line with our latest product offerings. Throughout the series we’ll provide use cases, differentiators and value based outcomes that address your customer’s needs to reduce operational overhead, control costs and increase security.
