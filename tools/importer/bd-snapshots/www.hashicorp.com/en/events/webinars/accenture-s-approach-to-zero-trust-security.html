Accenture's Approach To Zero Trust Security

During our cloud journey at Accenture we experienced, first-hand, the power of secrets management in the cloud at Accenture's size, scale, and complexity.

With 95% of our applications now in the cloud, we have found that there is no element of security that cloud solutions cannot support.

Join our session at the Zero Trust Security Virtual Series with partners on May 25th, 2022 to learn about the guiding principles we used to achieve security in the cloud operating model with HashiCorp Vault.
